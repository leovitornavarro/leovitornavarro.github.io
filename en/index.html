Leo Vitor Navarro

I work on phonology, morphology, and their interfaces, with a focus on Kadiwéu and Brazilian Portuguese.

My research combines linguistic description, formal analysis, experiments, and computational methods. I am particularly interested in morphophonology, nominal number, prosodic structure, and the analysis of Indigenous languages of Brazil.

Research

Kadiwéu nominal number and morphophonology

Description and analysis of singular–plural alternations in Kadiwéu (Guaicuruan), including plural allomorphy, stem alternations, vowel shortening, and the interaction between morphological and phonological conditioning.

M.A. thesis: Alomorfia de plural em kadiwéu · University of Campinas · 2026

Prosodic organization in Brazilian Portuguese

Experimental work on rhythmic and metrical organization in Brazilian Portuguese, including click-location data used to evaluate predictions about binary and ternary prosodic structure.

Computational approaches to linguistic analysis

Use of computational tools for morphological discovery, corpus organization, quantitative analysis, and formal phonological modeling, including MaxEnt and unsupervised morphology workflows.
